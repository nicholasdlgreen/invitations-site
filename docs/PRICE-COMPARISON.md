# Wedding invitations — what everyone charges

Measured 27 September 2026 by reading each site's own live calculator, except
Papier which is carried over from the 20 September workbook. Our own figures are
read from the published catalogue.

**Our margin is still 0, so every Foreverprint number below is our COST.** That
is the point of the exercise: the gap between our column and everyone else's is
the room available.

---

## The like-for-like: A5, single sided, 300gsm Tintoretto Gesso

The cleanest comparison in the whole exercise, because printed.com's default
wedding invitation uses **the same Fedrigoni paper we stock**.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Foreverprint (cost)** | £40.80 | £7.20 | **£48.00** | — |
| printed.com | £51.99 ex VAT | free | **£62.39** | **+30%** |

| Quantity | Foreverprint card+env (cost) | printed.com inc VAT | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | £36.00 | £38.95 | +8% |
| 100 | £48.00 | £62.39 | +30% |

printed.com's premium widens with quantity, which is the opposite of what a
volume discount looks like. On the same paper, at 100, there is **£14.39 of room
before we are level with them**.

## Four things this says about our pricing

**1. Our own supplier undercuts us on the plain stock.** PrintedEasy sell the
same Silk 300 A5 direct to consumers at £29 for 100 — we pay £23.20 for it. That
is a 25% gross margin if we matched them exactly, and they are one search away.
We cannot win a plain-silk price fight with our own supplier, and should not try.

**2. The luxury stocks are where the money is, and we already stock them.**
printed.com charge 83% more for Gesso than for silk (£0.62 vs £0.34). We pay 76%
more (£0.41 vs £0.23). The premium the market accepts for a Fedrigoni paper is
real and roughly matches the cost step, so it carries margin cleanly.

**3. Nobody charges for design, so the card price has to carry it.** Confirmed
again: Papier and Vistaprint sell their own designs and upload-your-own at the
same price. Our AI studio has to be paid for inside the card price.

**4. Envelopes are table stakes.** Included at printed.com ("our White Envelopes
are free"), instantprint (forced in, cannot be removed) and Papier. We charge
£7.20 per 100 A5 as an extra. Quoting a card-only price against their
envelope-inclusive one flatters us by about 15% and will read as a nasty
surprise at the basket.

## Traps that would corrupt this comparison

- **VAT.** Trade printers quote ex VAT, consumer sites inc VAT — a 20% swing.
  printed.com's figures above are ex VAT and have been grossed up; instantprint's
  are inc.
- **PrintedEasy's calculator defaults to VAT not payable**, because UK VAT
  zero-rates leaflets. **Invitations are standard-rated.** If VAT is due on what
  we buy, our true cost is 20% above every Foreverprint figure here. This is
  still unresolved and it moves every number in this document.
- **Turnaround.** instantprint's ladder has three tiers; the cheapest is used
  here. Their express price for the same job is £37.81, not £29.29.
- **Standing discounts.** Vistaprint, Zazzle and Optimalprint almost always have
  an offer running, so their list prices are close to fiction.
- **Compare the competitor's wedding line, not their cheapest sheet of card.**
  This was got wrong on 20 September and understated four sites.

## What is not measured

- **Papier** is carried from 20 September. Their current pages are heavily gated
  and would not yield a live figure.
- **Vistaprint** is the £0.70 headline rate; their quantity ladder was not read.
- Folded invitations, foiling and the other sizes. Foiling is worth doing next:
  printed.com charge **£136.96 ex VAT** for 100 foiled A5, against £51.99 plain —
  a **+163%** uplift for a finish that costs us a flat fee.
