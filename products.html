FAQs

Common questions.

What's your turnaround time?+
[REPLACE: Standard turnaround is 5-7 working days from approval of your digital proof. Rush orders can be quoted on request.]
Can I order samples first?+
Yes — free sample packs are available for all paper stocks. We post them out for free so you can feel the paper before placing your order.
What file formats do you accept?+
We accept PDF, AI (Adobe Illustrator), InDesign (.indd), and high-resolution PNG or JPG (300dpi minimum). PDF is preferred for best results.
Do I get a proof before printing?+
Yes. Every order includes a free digital proof. We won't print until you've approved the proof — so you can spot any errors before they're set in print.
What if my artwork has issues?+
Our team reviews every file. If we spot anything (low resolution, wrong colour mode, missing bleed), we'll flag it before printing and help you fix it.
Do you offer design services?+
Yes. If you don't have artwork ready, our Design Studio walks you through creating an invitation from scratch. Try it here →
Do you ship outside the UK?+
[REPLACE: Yes, we ship internationally. International shipping is calculated at checkout based on your destination and order weight.]
Can I change my order after placing it?+
Once you've approved the digital proof, your order goes to print. Before that, you can adjust quantities, papers or details — just contact us. After approval, changes may incur a reprint fee.
