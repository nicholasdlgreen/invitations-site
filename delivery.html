How It Works

All orders are dispatched via tracked courier once your order has been printed. You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order is on its way.

Delivery times run from the day your order is placed. We check every file before it goes to press and will contact you if anything needs attention.

We currently deliver to addresses within the United Kingdom only. If you require international delivery, please contact us before placing your order.

Packaging

Your invitations are carefully packaged to arrive in perfect condition. All orders are wrapped in tissue paper and packed in a rigid box to prevent damage in transit. We take great care to ensure that what you receive looks exactly as you approved on screen.
