Common questions

Find your answer.

How long does delivery take?+
Orders are printed within 3–5 working days, then sent by DPD tracked delivery — typically arriving 1–2 working days after dispatch. You'll get a tracking link once your order is on its way.
Is there a minimum order?+
Our minimum order is 25 cards. There's no maximum — and the more you order, the lower the price per card.
What paper stocks do you offer?+
We print on premium stocks from 300gsm upwards — our smooth signature white, plus luxurious cotton rag and textured options. You choose your paper when you personalise your design.
Can I add finishing touches like foil?+
Yes — you can add finishing touches such as gold foil, deckle edges, vellum wraps and wax seals when you personalise your design in the studio.
Can I see my design before I order?+
Yes — you'll see your design on screen as you personalise it, so you know exactly how it looks before you place your order.
Do you print my own artwork?+
Absolutely. With Upload & Print you can upload your own finished artwork and we'll print it on your chosen paper. Or use our Design Studio to create something from scratch.
What if something's wrong with my order?+
We're sorry if anything isn't right. Because everything is personalised, please get in touch with the team as soon as possible — chat with Lily below or email hello@foreverprint.com and we'll put it right.
How do I track my order?+
Once your order is dispatched you'll receive a DPD tracking link by email. You can also visit our Track Order page with your order number and email address.
