Common questions

Things people ask us.

Can I make changes to my order?+
Contact us as soon as you can, quoting your order reference. We can refund in full right up until your file goes to press. Once printing has begun a personalised order cannot be refunded.
What is the minimum order quantity?+
There is no minimum — you can order as few as you need. Pricing scales proportionally and is shown clearly before you commit.
What file formats do you accept?+
We accept PDF, AI (Adobe Illustrator), InDesign, and high-resolution PNG or JPG (300dpi minimum). PDF is preferred for best results — ideally exported in CMYK with 3mm bleed on all sides.
Do envelopes come included?+
Matching envelopes can be added at checkout, in a range of colours. They are priced per envelope and shown in your basket before you pay.
Do you deliver outside the UK?+
We currently deliver to UK addresses as standard. If you require international delivery, please contact us at hello@foreverprint.com before placing your order.
